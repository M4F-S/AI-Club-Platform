05 / Why we exist

AI is becoming a core skill — for coding, for building, for thinking.

But skill without judgment is just faster mistakes.

So we learn the tools and their limits. The power and the responsibility.

Peer to peer. Node to node. That's the network.

07 / Responsible by design

Slower signal.
Clearer thinking.

The field quiets down here — deliberately. Ethics isn't a checkbox on this network; it's a standing discussion with its own rhythm.

Privacy first

We respect 42 Berlin's community standards and privacy expectations — no scraping, no shady data, ever.

Limits out loud

Every workshop names what models can't do. Hallucination, bias, and failure modes are part of the syllabus.

No spam, no shilling

The club is never a marketing channel. Vendor-neutral means every tool earns its place on merit.
