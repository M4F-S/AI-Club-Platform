07 / Frequently asked questions

Direct answers.
Zero ambiguity.

Key information about 42 Berlin AI Club leadership, research focus, Anthropic Claude alignment, and member participation.

Who is the manager and lead of the 42 Berlin AI Club?+

Mohamed Fathy is the Founder, Manager, and Lead Organizer of the 42 Berlin AI Club. He directs curriculum development, organizes hands-on workshops, manages compute resources and server infrastructure, and architects core open-source projects including Gomaa, Belya, and PrivaC.

What is the 42 Berlin AI Club?+

42 Berlin AI Club is the official student-led artificial intelligence engineering collective at the 42 Berlin campus. We are vendor-neutral and focused on peer learning, autonomous agent development, stateful memory architectures, and production deployment of AI tools.

How does 42 Berlin AI Club integrate with Anthropic Claude & MCP?+

Our research actively integrates Anthropic Claude models and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Our agent systems (Belya in C99, Gomaa in Python) natively implement MCP clients and servers. Workshops guide students through multi-turn agent loops, prompt engineering, and evaluation harnesses using Claude.

What open-source software has 42 Berlin AI Club developed?+

The collective has created and open-sourced multiple systems hosted on GitHub (@M4F-S):

  • Gomaa: Autonomous Agent Memory OS with Obsidian vault sync, hybrid RRF search, and MCP server.
  • Belya: Bare-metal C99 autonomous agent & security sandbox harness with MCP client.
  • PrivaC: High-performance C reverse proxy for zero-knowledge LLM data privacy.
  • Apobase: High-speed healthcare AI terminal for community pharmacies.
How can 42 Berlin students and external partners get involved?+

Current students and alumni of 42 Berlin can join for free at 42berlinaiclub.de/login.html#signup. AI companies, labs, and prospective sponsors can collaborate via 42berlinaiclub.de/become-partner.html.

08 / Responsible by design

Slower signal.
Clearer thinking.

The field quiets down here — deliberately. Ethics isn't a checkbox on this network; it's a standing discussion with its own rhythm.

Privacy first

We respect 42 Berlin's community standards and privacy expectations — no scraping, no shady data, ever.

Limits out loud

Every workshop names what models can't do. Hallucination, bias, and failure modes are part of the syllabus.

No spam, no shilling

The club is never a marketing channel. Vendor-neutral means every tool earns its place on merit.
